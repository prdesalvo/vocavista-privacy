The words you actually need

Learn wordsfrom your own life.

The things in your kitchen, what people say at the market, the phrases for the trip you’re planning. Every word becomes a card with a memory trick you can picture.

Made for iPhone and iPad. Also on Mac with Apple silicon.

Take a look around ↓

Discover

Words for where you’ll be.

Name a situation, like a café, a doctor’s visit or a train station, and get a short scene at your level with the useful words listed. Ask how to say something, then save what you need.

Photos

Point at your world.

Take a picture of a room, a menu or a sign. Vocavista suggests the words in it, reads any text, and offers phrases you could use there.

Memory tricks

Cards that stick.

Each card has its meaning, an example, a pronunciation guide and a sound-alike story to picture. Start with illustrated Spanish starter packs, or shape a card in a short chat.

Practice that spaces itself

See it, say it, then let it rest.

Recall each word from its meaning and picture, check the trick, and rate how it went. Vocavista schedules the next review, and Progress shows your streak and which words are getting stronger.

Private by design

Your words stay yours.

No account, ads or tracking. Your Library lives on your device and in your own iCloud account. Apple Intelligence runs on your device, and you choose which features may use Apple’s Private Cloud Compute.

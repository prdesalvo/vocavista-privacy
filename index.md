# Vocavista Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

Vocavista helps you learn the words of a new language from your own life: scenes you ask for, photos you take, and cards with memory tricks. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your words live on your device and in your own iCloud account, and we never see them.

## What we collect: nothing.

Vocavista has no accounts of its own, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We (the developer) cannot see your words, cards, photos, scenes, or practice history.

## Where your information is kept

What you add (your words and cards, their pictures, your scenes and conversations, and your practice history) is stored on your device and synced through your own private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it can appear on your other devices. Apple handles that storage under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Apple Intelligence on your device

On devices that support it, Vocavista uses Apple's on-device language model to write practice scenes, look up words, suggest memory tricks, and describe your photos. That processing happens on your device. Translation, text recognition in photos, and speech recognition also use Apple's on-device features.

## Optional Apple Private Cloud Compute

On supported devices running iOS 27 or later, some features can use Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Each has its own setting, saved only on the device where you set it:

- **Discover** (off unless you turn it on): sends your message, the conversation so far, your learning language and scene settings.
- **Word cards** (off unless you turn it on): sends the card, your messages, the conversation, picture labels and the results of changes made in the chat.
- **Photo details** (on by default): sends the photo you choose, with its metadata removed, and your learning language, to describe it and suggest words and phrases. Quick words, text recognition and translation still run on your device. You can turn it off in Settings or in Photo options.

Apple uses what is sent to fulfill the request and does not retain it; the developer never receives it. Read [Apple Intelligence & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/intelligence-engine/). The app keeps a count of its own cloud requests on your device, without their content.

## Camera, photos, and microphone

- **Camera:** take a photo to learn its words or read its text.
- **Photos:** Vocavista uses Apple's photo picker, so it sees only the photos you choose. If you allow access, it also shows your most recent photo on the album button. Photos you learn from are kept with your Library.
- **Microphone and speech recognition:** say what you want instead of typing it. Your speech is transcribed on your device and the recording is deleted once it is transcribed.

You can change any of these permissions in iOS Settings.

## Pictures you create

When you make a picture for a card with Apple's Image Playground, Apple creates it under its own terms, and the result is saved with your card.

## Files you import

When you import a word list, the file is read on your device and the words you choose are added to your Library.

## Children

Vocavista collects nothing from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your information

Delete words, scenes, or photos in the app (deleted words and scenes wait in Recently Deleted until you remove them for good), or delete the app and remove its data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
